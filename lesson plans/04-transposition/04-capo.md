# Capo Logic for Guitar and Mandolin

**Track:** transposition | **Difficulty:** 3/10 | **Time:** ~8 min

## Overview

A capo raises pitch without changing chord shapes.

## How it works

G shapes + capo 2 = A major. Capo 3 from G = B♭. Capo 5 from G = C. Each fret = one semitone.

## Why use it

Open-chord resonance and easier fingerings in keys that would otherwise require barre chords. Balance against vocal range.

## Communication

Tell the band 'capo 2, G shapes in A' to avoid mismatch with concert-pitch instruments.

## Quiz Questions

### Q1. G shapes, capo fret 2:

- A) A major
- B) G major
- C) B major
- D) E major

**Answer:** A

### Q2. Capo preserves open-chord fingerings in higher keys.

**Answer:** True
