# Transposing Melodies by Interval

**Track:** transposition | **Difficulty:** 3/10 | **Time:** ~8 min

## Overview

Move every note by the same interval — up a tone from D to E major, down a fourth from G to D.

## Scale-degree method

If a phrase starts on the 5th of G (D), the same phrase in D major starts on A (5th of D). Faster than note-by-note for experienced players.

## Accidentals

Move accidentals with their notes. C♮ in F major becomes D♮ when transposing up a whole step to G — not C♯ unless harmony demands it.

## Quiz Questions

### Q1. G major up a major second:

- A) A major
- B) D major
- C) F major
- D) E minor only

**Answer:** A

### Q2. Move every note including accidentals by the same interval.

**Answer:** True
