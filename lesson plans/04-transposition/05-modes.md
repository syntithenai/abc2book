# Modes and Modal Transposition

**Track:** transposition | **Difficulty:** 7/10 | **Time:** ~8 min

## Overview

Modal transposition preserves characteristic altered scale degrees, not just letter names.

## Key signatures

D dorian shares C major's signature. G mixolydian shares C major's signature. D major has two sharps — different collection.

## Preserving colour

Transposing D mixolydian to E mixolydian: keep the flat 7th (D natural in E mixolydian). Dorian keeps major 6th; mixolydian keeps flat 7th.

## Quiz Questions

### Q1. D dorian key signature:

- A) C major (none)
- B) D major (2 sharps)
- C) F major (1 flat)
- D) G major (1 sharp)

**Answer:** A

### Q2. Mixolydian lowers the 7th vs major.

**Answer:** True
