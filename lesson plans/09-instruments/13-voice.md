# Voice as Instrument

**Track:** instruments | **Difficulty:** 2/10 | **Time:** ~12 min

## Overview

The human voice is the oldest instrument — sung, spoken, and shouted across every culture. Classified as aerophone (lungs provide air; vocal folds vibrate).

## Registers

Chest voice (modal register), head voice, falsetto, whistle register. Passaggio — transition points between registers.

## Voice types

Soprano, mezzo, alto/contralto, tenor, baritone, bass — choral classification. Folk singing ignores Fach categories; find comfortable range.

## Styles

Opera bel canto. Gospel and blues growl. Sean-nós unaccompanied Irish song. Shape-note hymnody. Throat singing (Tuva, Mongolia).

## Health

Hydration, warm-up, rest. Hoarseness lasting >2 weeks warrants medical attention.

## Quiz Questions

- A) Vocal fold vibration
