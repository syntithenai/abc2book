# Bodhrán and Percussion

**Track:** instruments | **Difficulty:** 2/10 | **Time:** ~12 min

## Overview

Percussion: sound from striking, shaking, or scraping. Definite pitch (timpani, xylophone) or indefinite (drum, cymbal).

## Bodhrán

Irish frame drum — goatskin head, wooden rim, crossbar handle inside. Played with double-ended tipper (beater). Introduced widely to trad in mid-20th century; controversial if overused in sessions.

## Technique

Hand on inside of head controls pitch and damping. Kerry style vs West Limerick style. Not traditional in all regions — respect session culture.

## Other trad percussion

Spoons, bones, tambourine. Cajón in fusion. Orchestral: timpani, snare, bass drum, cymbals, triangle.

## Quiz Questions

### Q1. Bodhrán is:

- A) Irish frame drum
- B) Brass horn
- C) Plucked string
- D) Keyboard

**Answer:** A

### Q2. Bodhrán player may dampen pitch with hand inside the head.

**Answer:** True

### Q3. Tipper is:

- A) Beater for bodhrán
- B) Tuning peg
- C) Reed for clarinet
- D) Piano pedal

**Answer:** A
