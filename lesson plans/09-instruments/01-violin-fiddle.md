# Violin and Fiddle

**Track:** instruments | **Difficulty:** 2/10 | **Time:** ~12 min

## Overview

The violin is a bowed string instrument with four strings tuned in fifths: G-D-A-E. 'Fiddle' denotes the same instrument in folk and traditional contexts — technique and repertoire differ, not the tool.

## Construction

Spruce top, maple back and sides, ebony fingerboard. Soundpost and bass bar transmit vibrations. Baroque violins used gut strings and lower tension; modern setup uses steel or synthetic strings and higher bridge.

## Playing technique

Bowing produces sustained tone; left-hand stops define pitch. Ornamentation in Irish trad includes cuts, rolls, and slides. Scottish style emphasises strong bowing and drone strings.

## Range and role

G3 to E7+ depending on player. Lead melody in sessions; harmony in twin-fiddle arrangements. Hardanger fiddle (Norway) adds sympathetic strings.

## Quiz Questions

### Q1. Standard violin tuning (low to high):

- A) G-D-A-E
- B) E-A-D-G
- C) D-A-D-A
- D) C-G-C-G

**Answer:** A

### Q2. Fiddle and violin are the same instrument.

**Answer:** True — Different repertoire and style, same construction.

### Q3. Violin belongs to Hornbostel-Sachs class:

- A) Chordophone (bowed)
- B) Aerophone
- C) Membranophone
- D) Idiophone

**Answer:** A
