# Accordion and Concertina

**Track:** instruments | **Difficulty:** 2/10 | **Time:** ~12 min

## Overview

Free-reed aerophones: air vibrates metal reeds through bellows pressure. Accordion has keyboard or button right hand + bass buttons left. Concertina is smaller, hexagonal, fully buttoned.

## Types

Piano accordion (keyboard RH). Button accordion — chromatic or diatonic systems. Irish button accordion often B/C or C♯/D tuned. Anglo concertina: push/pull different notes per button.

## In trad

Céilí band staple — melody and chord accompaniment. Joe Burke, Sharon Shannon, Máirtín O'Connor represent Irish accordion mastery. Box and button are session synonyms in Ireland.

## Mechanism

Bellows direction changes air flow; valves and reeds produce pitch. Musette tuning (slightly detuned reeds) gives characteristic shimmer in continental styles.

## Quiz Questions

### Q1. Accordion sound comes from:

- A) Free reeds vibrated by bellows air
- B) Bowed strings
- C) Hammered strings
- D) Lip vibration only

**Answer:** A

### Q2. Anglo concertina has different notes on push vs pull.

**Answer:** True

### Q3. Common Irish accordion tuning pairs include:

- A) B/C and C♯/D
- B) E-A-D-G guitar tuning
- C) G-D-A-E violin tuning
- D) No standard tunings

**Answer:** A
