# Mandolin and Banjo

**Track:** instruments | **Difficulty:** 2/10 | **Time:** ~12 min

## Overview

Both instruments anchor American old-time and bluegrass alongside Celtic sessions abroad.

## Mandolin

Eight strings in four courses tuned G-D-A-E (like violin). Plucked with plectrum. Italian origin; Orville Gibson Americanised design. Tremolo technique sustains long notes.

## Banjo

African origin via Caribbean. Drum-like head with stretched membrane; strings over a bridge. 5-string (bluegrass, clawhammer frailing) and 4-string (tenor banjo for Irish trad — tuned like mandola/viola).

## In sessions

Tenor banjo provides chordal/melodic drive in Irish céilí bands. Mandolin doubles fiddle melodies an octave below or provides chord backup.
