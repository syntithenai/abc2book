# Guitar

**Track:** instruments | **Difficulty:** 2/10 | **Time:** ~12 min

## Overview

The guitar is a fretted chordophone — six strings standard (E-A-D-G-B-E low to high), though seven, eight, and twelve-string variants exist. Origins trace to medieval lute family; modern classical guitar crystallised in 19th-century Spain.

## Types

Classical (nylon strings, fingerstyle). Steel-string acoustic (folk, country). Electric (magnetic pickups, amplification). Resonator (Dobro) for slide. Flamenco guitar — lighter, tap plate.

## Technique

Right hand: fingerpicking (p-i-m-a) or plectrum. Left hand: frets fix pitch; barre chords across strings. Capo transposes open shapes.

## In trad

Backing chords in sessions; DADGAD and open tunings for Celtic accompaniment. Flatpicking for reels; fingerstyle for airs.
