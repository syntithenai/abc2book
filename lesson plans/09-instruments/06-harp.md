# Harp

**Track:** instruments | **Difficulty:** 2/10 | **Time:** ~12 min

## Overview

The harp is among the oldest instruments — depicted in ancient Egypt and Mesopotamia. Sound from plucked strings; frame holds tension. Pedal harp (modern orchestra) and lever/folk harp (trad) serve different roles.

## Irish harp

Wire-strung cláirseach historically; modern Irish harp (neo-Irish) nylon strings, levers for semitone changes. National symbol of Ireland. Turlough O'Carolan (1670–1738) composed harp music still central to trad repertoire.

## Concert harp

47 strings, seven pedals each altering pitch by semitone. Complex mechanism. Composers: Debussy (*Danse sacrée et danse profane*), Ravel.

## Technique

Pluck near middle of string for warmest tone. Harmonics, pres de la table (near soundboard) effects. Left hand bass, right hand melody in classical; both hands melodic in trad.
