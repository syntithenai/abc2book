# Debussy: Colour, Modes, and New Sonorities

**Track:** history | **Difficulty:** 5/10 | **Time:** ~10 min

## Overview

Claude Debussy (1862–1918) broke from Germanic functional harmony toward colour, parallel motion, and modal scales — founder of musical Impressionism.

## Key works

*Prélude à l'après-midi d'un faune* (1894) treats chords as sonorities. *Clair de lune*, *La mer*, *Children's Corner*. Influenced film music and contemporary folk arranging.

## Scales

Whole-tone and pentatonic collections overlap with Celtic and Asian traditions. Avoids heavy V–I cadences — parallels modal Irish melody.

## Voicing

Wide spacing, soft doublings, planing parallel triads. Colour when tunes 'float' without demanding classical cadences.

## Quiz Questions

### Q1. Debussy's harmony favours:

- A) Colour over strict functional progression
- B) Bach fugues exclusively
- C) Twelve-tone rows only
- D) Brass band unison

**Answer:** A
