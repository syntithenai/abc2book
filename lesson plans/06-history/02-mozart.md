# Mozart: Clarity, Balance, and Melody

**Track:** history | **Difficulty:** 3/10 | **Time:** ~10 min

## Overview

Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart (1756–1791) composed symphonies, operas, chamber music, and piano works with effortless melodic balance — the epitome of Classical style.

## Contributions

Over 600 works including operas *The Marriage of Figaro*, *Don Giovanni*, *The Magic Flute*. Piano concertos and symphonies define Classical form. Child prodigy who toured Europe before age 10.

## Phrasing model

Clear four-bar antecedent/consequent structure. Alberti bass and I–V–I motion underpin countless songs. Lift at midpoint cadence, settle at authentic cadence.

## For trad players

Mozart's phrasing helps shape airs and slow session tunes with intentional breathing points.
