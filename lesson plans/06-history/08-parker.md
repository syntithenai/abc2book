# Charlie Parker: Bebop and Harmonic Velocity

**Track:** history | **Difficulty:** 6/10 | **Time:** ~10 min

## Overview

Charlie 'Bird' Parker (1920–1955) revolutionised alto saxophone with bebop — fast harmonic rhythm, chromatic lines, improvisation on chord changes.

## Compositions

*Confirmation*, *Blues for Alice*, *Ko-Ko*, *Ornithology* (based on *How High the Moon*). Tunes became études for jazz musicians worldwide.

## Practice ethic

Legendary woodshedding — 11–15 hours daily in youth. Oral tradition plus rigorous technique produces new vocabulary.

## For trad improvisers

Target chord tones on strong beats; chromatic approaches between. Apply sparingly on modal tunes.
