# Chord Quality by Ear

**Track:** ear-training | **Difficulty:** 4/10 | **Time:** ~10 min

## Overview

Identifying major, minor, diminished, and seventh chords by ear accelerates session backup and transcription.

## Triad quality

Major: bright, stable. Minor: darker, melancholic. Diminished: tense, unstable. Augmented: dreamy, unresolved.

## Seventh chords

Dominant 7th: bluesy tension, wants to resolve. Major 7th: jazzy, sophisticated. Minor 7th: mellow, modal.

## Bass note

Same chord quality sounds different with different bass — train ear to hear root vs inversion.

## Progressions

I–IV–V–I loop — recognise by bass movement and overall colour shift. ii–V–I in jazz.

## Quiz Questions

### Q1. Minor triad sounds compared to major:

- A) Darker/more melancholic
- B) Identical
- C) Always dissonant
- D) Only percussion

**Answer:** A

### Q2. Dominant 7th chord creates tension wanting resolution.

**Answer:** True

### Q3. Chord inversions share quality but differ in bass note.

**Answer:** True
