# Rhythm Dictation

**Track:** ear-training | **Difficulty:** 3/10 | **Time:** ~10 min

## Overview

Rhythm dictation trains you to notate or reproduce rhythmic patterns heard — essential for learning tunes by ear.

## Subdivision

Feel beat first, then subdivide: eighths in 4/4, triplets in 6/8. Tap foot on main beat only.

## Patterns

Start with 2-bar patterns: quarter and eighth combinations. Add syncopation. Jig patterns: long-short-short or three equal eighths per beat.

## Notation

Use correct beam grouping — eighths beamed per beat in 4/4; in 6/8, beam three eighths per beat.

## Practice

Clap back rhythms before writing. Slow recordings to 75% speed. Bodhrán and metronome apps help.

## Quiz Questions

### Q1. In 6/8, main beats are felt as:

- A) Two beats per bar (compound duple)
- B) Six equal beats
- C) Three quarter-note beats
- D) One beat only

**Answer:** A

### Q2. Tap foot on main beat when dictating rhythm.

**Answer:** True

### Q3. Syncopation stresses:

- A) Weak beats or offbeats
- B) Only beat one always
- C) Silence only
- D) Every bar line

**Answer:** A
