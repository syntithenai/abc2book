# Interval Recognition

**Track:** ear-training | **Difficulty:** 3/10 | **Time:** ~10 min

## Overview

Hearing intervals accurately is the foundation of sight-singing, transcription, and harmonising by ear.

## Consonant intervals

Unison, P4, P5, P8 — stable. Major and minor 3rds and 6ths — sweet. Learn these first.

## Dissonant intervals

m2, M2, m7, M7, tritone — tense. Identify by tension level and melodic direction.

## Reference songs

M2 = *Happy Birthday* first two notes. m3 = *Greensleeves* start. M3 = *When the Saints*. P4 = *Here Comes the Bride*. P5 = *Star Wars* fanfare. M6 = *My Bonnie*. m7 = *Star Trek* opening (partial).

## Practice

Use apps (Functional Ear Trainer, Complete Ear Trainer). Sing intervals up and down from any note. Partner testing: one plays, one names.
