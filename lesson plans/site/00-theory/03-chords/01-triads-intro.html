Triads — Introduction

Track: theory | Tier: intro | Difficulty: 2/10 Prerequisites: Major Scales — Introduction Tags: triads, chords

Overview

A triad stacks two thirds above a root — the basic harmonic unit of Western and much folk music.

Qualities

  • Major: M3 + m3 (C-E-G)
  • Minor: m3 + M3 (A-C-E)
  • Diminished: m3 + m3 (B-D-F)
  • Augmented: M3 + M3 (C-E-G♯)

Roman numerals in major

I, ii, iii, IV, V, vi, vii° — capital = major, lowercase = minor, ° = diminished.
