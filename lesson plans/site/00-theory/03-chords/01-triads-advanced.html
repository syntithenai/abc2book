Triads — Applied Harmony and Colour

Track: theory | Tier: advanced | Difficulty: 6/10 Prerequisites: Triads — Applied Tags: triads, extensions, jazz

Overview

Beyond three-note triads: seventh chords, extensions, and jazz colour — still grounded in triad stacks.

Seventh chords

Add a seventh above the triad: major 7, dominant 7, minor 7, half-diminished. Dominant 7 (V7) drives cadences.

Extensions

9, 11, 13 — tertian stacks. In jazz and some folk fusion, upper extensions add colour without changing the underlying function.

Representative depth

You do not need every extension in every style. Irish session backup is often triadic; jazz standards use sevenths extensively — revisit in style units.
