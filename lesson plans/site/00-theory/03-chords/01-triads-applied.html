Triads — Applied

Track: theory | Tier: applied | Difficulty: 4/10 Prerequisites: Triads — Introduction Tags: triads, inversions, voice-leading

Overview

Triad inversions change bass note while keeping the same pitch classes. Voice-leading connects chords smoothly.

Inversions

  • Root position: root in bass (C-E-G)
  • First inversion: third in bass (E-G-C)
  • Second inversion: fifth in bass (G-C-E)

Figured bass: 5/3, 6, 6/4 — advanced tier in figured-bass lesson.

Voice-leading basics

Move voices by step when possible. Avoid parallel perfect fifths and octaves in strict counterpoint; in folk accompaniment, practicality often wins.
