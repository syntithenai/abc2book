Cadences — Introduction

Track: theory | Tier: intro | Difficulty: 3/10 Prerequisites: Triads — Introduction Tags: cadences, harmony

Overview

A cadence is a harmonic punctuation at a phrase end — the musical equivalent of a comma or full stop.

Common types

  • Perfect (authentic): V–I (or V7–I) — strong closure
  • Plagal: IV–I — “amen” cadence
  • Half: ends on V — question, continues
  • Deceptive: V–vi — surprise, continues
