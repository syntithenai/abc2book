Intervals — Advanced

Track: theory | Tier: advanced | Difficulty: 7/10 Prerequisites: Intervals — Applied Tags: intervals, microtonal, extended

Overview

Advanced interval study: enharmonic respelling, quarter-tone awareness (for world-music units), and analytical labels in scores.

Enharmonic equivalence

F♯ and G♭ are enharmonic in equal temperament — same pitch, different spelling depending on key context. Correct spelling follows diatonic scale membership.

Extended and altered intervals

Augmented and diminished intervals appear in chromatic harmony and jazz. Label the base size, then alter by semitone.

World music note

Many traditions use neutral seconds and thirds not captured by twelve-tone equal temperament. Regional lessons (Middle East, India) revisit intervals in those systems — Western labels remain your starting toolkit.
