Intervals — Applied

Track: theory | Tier: applied | Difficulty: 4/10 Prerequisites: Intervals — Introduction Tags: intervals, harmony, compound

Overview

Invert intervals, spell them in any key, and recognise compound intervals (beyond the octave) in repertoire.

Inversion

Invert an interval by moving the lower note up an octave (or lower the upper). Rule: simple intervals sum to 9 (third ↔ sixth). Major becomes minor and vice versa; perfect stays perfect.

Compound intervals

Ninth = octave + second. Eleventh = octave + fourth. Used in jazz extensions and some folk ornaments — label the simple interval first, then add octave.

Harmonic use

Major and minor thirds build triads (next chord tier). Perfect fifths stabilise; tritones create tension resolved in cadences.
