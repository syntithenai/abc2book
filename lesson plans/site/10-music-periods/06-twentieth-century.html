20th Century and Beyond

Track: music-periods | Difficulty: 4/10 | Time: ~12 min

Overview

20th century shattered and rebuilt musical language — jazz, serialism, minimalism, recording technology, global fusion.

Movements

Impressionism (Debussy, Ravel). Expressionism (Schoenberg, Webern). Neoclassicism (Stravinsky). Minimalism (Reich, Glass).

Technology

Phonograph (1877), radio, LP, cassette, CD, streaming — each transformed how music spreads. Alan Lomax to Spotify.

Trad revival

1960s–70s folk revivals globally. Seán Ó Riada, Bothy Band. Contemporary trad continues evolving with production and fusion.

Quiz Questions

Q1. Schoenberg pioneered:

  • A) Twelve-tone serialism
  • B) Gregorian chant
  • C) Baroque fugue only
  • D) Tin whistle making

Answer: A

Q2. Recording technology transformed music distribution.

Answer: True
