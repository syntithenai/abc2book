Baroque Era Overview (1600–1750)

Track: music-periods | Difficulty: 3/10 | Time: ~12 min

Overview

Baroque: birth of opera, oratorio, concerto, and tonality as we know it. Bach, Handel, Vivaldi, Corelli dominate.

Features

Basso continuo, terraced dynamics, doctrine of affections (specific emotions per movement). Equal temperament enabled all-key composition.

Forms

Fugue, concerto grosso, suite, opera seria. Vivaldi’s Four Seasons; Handel’s Messiah.

Trad link

Baroque dance rhythms feed into European folk dance; ornamentation practice influences traditional gracing.

Quiz Questions

Q1. Baroque basso continuo provides:

  • A) Harmonic foundation from bass line
  • B) Only percussion
  • C) Random noise
  • D) Lyrics only

Answer: A

Q2. Bach and Handel are Baroque composers.

Answer: True
