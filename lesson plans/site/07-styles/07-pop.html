Pop Song Craft: Hooks, Verse-Chorus, and Production

Track: styles | Difficulty: 3/10 | Time: ~10 min

Overview

Popular song favours memorable hooks, verse–chorus form, and repeating four-chord loops.

Chord loops

I–V–vi–IV and rotations (vi–IV–I–V) underpin countless hits. Folk singers borrow pop craft for originals and trad arrangements.

Production

Verse stripped to guitar-vocal; chorus adds bodhrán and fiddle. Dynamic build into final chorus. Pacing for festival slots.

Form

Verse narrates; chorus repeats hook. Bridge provides contrast before final chorus.

Quiz Questions

Q1. Common pop loop:

  • A) I–V–vi–IV
  • B) vii°–I only
  • C) IV repeated
  • D) Drone only

Answer: A

Q2. Verse–chorus alternates narrative and repeated hook.

Answer: True
