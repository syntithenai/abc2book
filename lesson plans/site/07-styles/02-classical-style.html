Classical Style: Balance and Sonata Form

Track: styles | Difficulty: 4/10 | Time: ~10 min

Overview

Classical era (c. 1750–1820) values symmetry, homophonic texture, and clearly articulated phrases.

Texture

Melody with accompaniment dominates over baroque polyphony. Alberti bass (broken chords) supports singable top lines.

Sonata form

Exposition (themes in home and dominant keys) → development (fragmentation, modulation) → recapitulation (themes in home key). Large-scale question-answer logic applies to short tunes too.

Ensemble

Haydn and Mozart string quartets model conversational listening — metaphor for good session etiquette.

Quiz Questions

Q1. Classical texture often features:

  • A) Clear melody with accompaniment
  • B) Unstructured noise
  • C) Only 7/8 meter
  • D) No phrases

Answer: A

Q2. Sonata form: exposition, development, recapitulation.

Answer: True
