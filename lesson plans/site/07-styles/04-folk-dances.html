Reels, Jigs, Waltzes, and Hornpipes

Track: styles | Difficulty: 2/10 | Time: ~10 min

Overview

Irish and Scottish session dance types each carry a distinct pulse — matching foot-tap to meter is essential session etiquette.

Reels

2/2 or 4/4, steady driving beat. ‘ONE-two THREE-four’ at moderate-fast tempo. Most common session dance type.

Jigs

Double jig: 6/8, two beats per bar of three eighths — ‘JIG-ged-y JIG-ged-y’. Single jig and slide: 12/8 variants regionally.

Waltzes and hornpipes

Waltz: 3/4, strong beat one. Hornpipe: often dotted long-short rhythm, 4/4, slower bouncy feel — regional variation in dotting.

Quiz Questions

Q1. Double jig notated in:

  • A) 6/8
  • B) 3/4 waltz
  • C) 5/4
  • D) 7/8 only

Answer: A

Q2. Reels use steady 2/2 or 4/4 drive.

Answer: True
