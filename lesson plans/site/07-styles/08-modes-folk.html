Modes in Folk Practice

Track: styles | Difficulty: 4/10 | Time: ~10 min

Overview

Modes explain tunes that don’t behave like textbook major or minor — central to authentic Irish and Scottish harmonisation.

Dorian

Minor 3rd, major 6th. A dorian: A B C D E F♯ G A. ‘Banish Misfortune’ often analysed as A dorian.

Mixolydian

Major 3rd, flat 7th. D mixolydian: D E F♯ G A B C D. Many D-centred tunes use C natural.

Harmonising

Don’t sharp the characteristic flat note. Mode-aware backup keeps sessions sounding authentic.

Quiz Questions

Q1. D dorian vs D natural minor:

  • A) Major sixth
  • B) Lowered third
  • C) No fourth
  • D) Extra sharps

Answer: A

Q2. Mixolydian lowers the 7th vs major.

Answer: True
