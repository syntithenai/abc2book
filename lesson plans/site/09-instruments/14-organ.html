Organ

Track: instruments | Difficulty: 3/10 | Time: ~12 min

Overview

The pipe organ forces air through pipes of varying length — one of the largest and oldest mechanical instruments. Church and concert hall icon.

Mechanism

Thousands of pipes; stops select which ranks sound. Manuals (keyboards) and pedalboard. Mechanical action (tracker) or electric/pneumatic.

Sound

Principals (flue pipes), flutes, reeds (trumpet stop), mixtures (multiple ranks). Enormous dynamic range — whisper to full tutti.

Composers

Bach (Toccata and Fugue in D minor), Franck, Widor, Messiaen. Baroque organ temperament and registration differ from Romantic symphonic organ.

Related

Harmonium (reed organ, pressure bellows). Theatre organ. Hammond organ (electromagnetic tonewheels) in jazz and rock.

Quiz Questions

Q1. Pipe organ sound from:

  • A) Air through pipes
  • B) Hammered strings
  • C) Bowed strings
  • D) Plucked nylon

Answer: A

Answer: True

  • C) Change time signature
  • D) Tune violin strings
