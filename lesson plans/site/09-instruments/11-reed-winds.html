Clarinet and Saxophone

Track: instruments | Difficulty: 3/10 | Time: ~12 min

Overview

Single-reed woodwinds: reed vibrates against mouthpiece. Despite ‘woodwind’ name, modern bodies are often plastic or metal.

Clarinet

Cylinder bore; cylindrical bore overblows at twelfth. B♭ clarinet most common. Wide dynamic range. Mozart and Brahms clarinet quintets. Klezmer and jazz traditions.

Saxophone

Adolphe Sax, 1840s Belgium. Conical bore overblows at octave. Family from soprano to baritone. Central to jazz — Charlie Parker (alto), John Coltrane (tenor).

Double reeds

Oboe and bassoon use two reeds bound together. Distinctive nasal oboe tone; bassoon’s wide range in orchestra.

Quiz Questions

Q1. Clarinet is:

  • A) Single-reed woodwind
  • B) Brass instrument
  • C) Bowed string
  • D) Percussion

Answer: A

Q2. Saxophone invented by Adolphe Sax.

Answer: True

Q3. Clarinet overblows at the:

  • A) Twelfth
  • B) Octave
  • C) Fifth
  • D) Unison only

Answer: A — Cylindrical bore property.
