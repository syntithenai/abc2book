Cello and Double Bass

Track: instruments | Difficulty: 2/10 | Time: ~12 min

Overview

Lower members of the violin family — essential in classical orchestra and increasingly present in creative trad arrangements.

Cello

Four strings C-G-D-A. Held between knees (endpin to floor). Rich tenor-bass range. Bach Cello Suites pinnacle of solo repertoire. In trad: accompaniment, drone, rhythmic pulse.

Double bass

E-A-D-G (often with extension or fifth string). Largest common bowed string. Jazz walking bass lines; orchestral foundation. Irish bass guitar sometimes substitutes in bands.

Technique

Bowing and pizzicato (plucked). Bass provides harmonic root in ensemble — voice-leading considerations apply.

Quiz Questions

Q1. Cello tuning (low to high):

  • A) C-G-D-A
  • B) G-D-A-E
  • C) E-A-D-G
  • D) D-A-D-A

Answer: A
