Brass: Trumpet and Trombone

Track: instruments | Difficulty: 3/10 | Time: ~12 min

Overview

Brass instruments produce sound by buzzing lips into a cup mouthpiece — air column resonates in tubular metal.

Trumpet

Three valves lower pitch by semitones. B♭ trumpet most common (transposing — written C sounds B♭). Natural trumpet (no valves) used in Baroque. Jazz, orchestral, and brass band repertoire.

Trombone

Slide changes tube length — no valves. Seven positions. Unique glissando capability. Sacred (Gabrieli) to jazz (J.J. Johnson) to ska.

French horn, tuba

Horn: coiled tubing, hand-stopping technique, mellow tone. Tuba: lowest brass, bass foundation in bands.

Quiz Questions

Q1. Trumpet pitch changed by:

  • A) Valves (and embouchure)
  • B) Frets
  • C) Keys like clarinet
  • D) Bowing

Answer: A

Q2. Trombone uses a slide not valves.

Answer: True

Q3. B♭ trumpet is a transposing instrument.

Answer: True — Written C sounds concert B♭.
