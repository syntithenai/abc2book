Piano

Track: instruments | Difficulty: 2/10 | Time: ~12 min

Overview

The piano is a keyboard chordophone where hammers strike strings. 88 keys (standard) span over seven octaves in equal temperament. Invented c. 1700 by Bartolomeo Cristofori in Italy.

Mechanism

Key depression launches felt hammer; damper stops vibration on release. Sustain pedal lifts all dampers. Cast-iron frame (19th century) withstands 20+ tons string tension.

Types

Grand piano: horizontal strings, superior control — concert halls. Upright: vertical strings, domestic and school use. Fortepiano (historical) has lighter touch and quicker decay.

In music

Solo repertoire from Bach to contemporary. Accompaniment for songs and céilí bands. Compositional tool — harmonic and contrapuntal possibilities in two hands. Cristofori solved hammer escapement; Steinway developed cross-stringing and duplex scaling.

  • B) Antonio Stradivari
  • C) Adolphe Sax
