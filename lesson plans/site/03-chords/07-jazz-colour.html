Altered Dominants and Jazz Colour Chords

Track: chords | Difficulty: 8/10 | Time: ~10 min

Overview

Altered dominants intensify V7 resolution through sharpened or flattened extensions.

Altered dominants

G7(♭9), G7(♯5) heighten tension before C major/minor. The altered scale (super Locrian) matches these tensions.

Tritone substitution

Replace G7 with D♭7 — shared tritone (F and B/C♭) allows similar resolution. Secondary dominants temporarily tonicise other degrees: A7→Dm in C is V7/vi.

Trad crossover

Recognise G7♯9 vs plain G7 in fusion settings. When unsure, play 3rd and 7th clearly.

Quiz Questions

Q1. Tritone sub for G7 in C:

  • A) D♭7
  • B) D7
  • C) B♭7
  • D) A7

Answer: A

Q2. Altered dominants modify 5th and/or 9th of V7.

Answer: True
