Chord Extensions: 9ths, 11ths, and 13ths

Track: chords | Difficulty: 6/10 | Time: ~10 min

Overview

Extensions continue stacking thirds above the seventh, adding colour tones.

Naming

9th = octave + 2nd. 11th = octave + 4th. 13th = octave + 6th. Cmaj9 = Cmaj7 + D. G13 might be voiced G-F-E-B, omitting some tones for playability.

Practice

Add extensions one at a time. Maj7 before maj9. Plain 7 before 9. Listen whether the added tone supports or fights the melody.

Voicing

Players drop the 5th or even root, keeping 3rd, 7th, and chosen extensions within hand span.

Quiz Questions

Q1. maj9 includes which interval above root (besides maj7)?

  • A) Major 9th
  • B) Minor 9th only
  • C) Perfect 11th without 7th
  • D) Augmented 4th only

Answer: A

Q2. Real-world voicings often omit some chord tones.

Answer: True
