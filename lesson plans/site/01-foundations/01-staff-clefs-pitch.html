The Staff, Clefs, and Pitch Names

Track: foundations | Difficulty: 0/10 | Time: ~8 min Tags: notation, pitch, clefs

Overview

Western music notation maps pitch onto a five-line staff. Each line and space represents a fixed pitch; notes above or below the staff use ledger lines. The system developed in medieval Europe and became standard by the 17th century, allowing complex polyphony to be written and preserved.

The treble clef (G clef)

The treble clef curls around the second line from the bottom, marking it as G above middle C. It is the default clef for violin, flute, tin whistle, mandolin, and most melody lines in tune books and ABC notation. Mnemonic for lines (bottom to top): E-G-B-D-F (“Every Good Boy Deserves Fudge”). Spaces spell F-A-C-E.

The bass clef (F clef)

The bass clef places F below middle C on the fourth line. It serves piano left hand, cello, bassoon, and tuba. Lines: G-B-D-F-A; spaces: A-C-E-G. When treble and bass staves are joined (grand staff), middle C sits on a ledger line between them — the bridge connecting the two clefs.

Pitch names and octaves

Pitch names cycle A through G. Middle C (C4 in scientific pitch notation) is the reference point near the centre of the piano keyboard. Each octave doubles the frequency: A4 = 440 Hz is the modern tuning standard. Notes an octave apart share a letter name; register numbers (C4, C5) distinguish them.

In practice

Trad musicians reading ABC or printed collections encounter treble clef almost exclusively. Bass clef matters for piano accompaniment and cello parts in céilí bands. Learning one clef thoroughly is enough to start; add the other when your instrument or role demands it.

Quiz Questions

Q1. Which clef is most commonly used for fiddle and whistle melody lines?

  • A) Treble clef (G clef)
  • B) Bass clef (F clef)
  • C) Alto clef
  • D) Percussion clef

Answer: A — Treble clef is standard for high-pitched melody instruments in tune books.

Q2. Middle C appears on a ledger line between treble and bass staves in piano notation.

Answer: True — In grand staff notation, middle C bridges the two clefs.

Q3. The spaces of the treble clef staff spell which word (bottom to top)?

  • A) FACE
  • B) EGBDF
  • C) ACEG
  • D) GBDFA

Answer: A — The four spaces from bottom to top are F, A, C, E.

Q4. Scientific pitch notation labels middle C as:

  • A) C4
  • B) C3
  • C) C5
  • D) C0

Answer: A — C4 is the international standard label for middle C.
