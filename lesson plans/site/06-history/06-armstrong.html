Louis Armstrong: Swing, Phrasing, and Improvisation

Track: history | Difficulty: 4/10 | Time: ~10 min

Overview

Louis Armstrong (1901–1971) transformed jazz from New Orleans collective improvisation into virtuoso solo trumpet and vocal art.

Recordings

Hot Five and Hot Seven sessions (1920s) set templates for swing, blues inflection, and improvising on changes. West End Blues opening cadenza remains iconic.

Swing

Rhythmic feel placing emphasis flexibly within the beat — not metronomic equality. ‘Playing behind the beat’ then catching up.

Storytelling

Simple start, build intensity, memorable ending — applies to variation on the repeat in dance music.

Quiz Questions

Q1. Armstrong pioneered on:

  • A) Trumpet and voice
  • B) Harpsichord
  • C) Uilleann pipes
  • D) Sitar

Answer: A

Q2. Swing subdivides the beat with bounce and syncopation.

Answer: True
