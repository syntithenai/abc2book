Duke Ellington: Band Colour and Composition

Track: history | Difficulty: 5/10 | Time: ~10 min

Overview

Edward Kennedy ‘Duke’ Ellington (1899–1974) led one of history’s greatest big bands, composing thousands of works treating each section as a colour palette.

Masterworks

Mood Indigo, Take the A Train, Black, Brown and Beige, Such Sweet Thunder. Wrote for specific players — Ben Webster, Johnny Hodges, Cootie Williams.

Orchestration

Same chord, different voicing = different room. Céilí bands borrow sectional thinking for accordion vs fiddle vs brass countermelodies.

Arranging

Vary texture each turn. Solo space over rich backgrounds. Shout chorus for climax — transferable to festival sets.

Quiz Questions

Q1. Ellington known as:

  • A) Bandleader and jazz orchestra composer
  • B) Baroque organ specialist only
  • C) Heavy-metal guitarist
  • D) Silent-film projectionist

Answer: A

Q2. Ellington treated band sections as distinct colours.

Answer: True
