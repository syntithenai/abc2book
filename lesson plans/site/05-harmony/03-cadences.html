Cadences: Full, Half, Plagal, and Deceptive

Track: harmony | Difficulty: 5/10 | Time: ~10 min

Overview

Cadences are harmonic punctuation at phrase endings.

Types

Authentic (PAC/IAC): V→I — full stop. Half: ends on V — comma, question. Plagal: IV→I — ‘amen’ cadence. Deceptive: V→vi — surprise.

In dance tunes

A part often half-cadences at midpoint; B part authentic-cadences home. AABB structure maps to these patterns.

Accompaniment

Don’t push dominant too early — steals the half cadence’s lift.

Quiz Questions

Q1. V→I at phrase end:

  • A) Authentic cadence
  • B) Half cadence
  • C) Deceptive cadence
  • D) Plagal only

Answer: A

Q2. Half cadence ends on V.

Answer: True
