Phrase Structure in Tunes and Songs

Track: harmony | Difficulty: 4/10 | Time: ~10 min

Overview

Phrases are coherent melodic units, usually 4 or 8 bars, ending with cadences.

Grouping

Irish dance tunes: two-bar cells → eight-bar parts. Songs: antecedent (question) + consequent (answer) phrases.

Harmonic rhythm

How often chords change shapes feel. One chord per bar vs one chord for four bars creates different energy.

Analysis skill

Map phrases to find breathe points, variation spots, and percussion turns.

Quiz Questions

Q1. Eight-bar strain often divides into:

  • A) Two four-bar phrases
  • B) Eight one-bar phrases
  • C) One sixteen-bar phrase
  • D) Three three-bar phrases

Answer: A

Q2. Phrase endings align with cadences.

Answer: True
