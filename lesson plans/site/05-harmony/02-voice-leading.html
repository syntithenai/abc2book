Voice Leading Basics

Track: harmony | Difficulty: 5/10 | Time: ~10 min

Overview

Voice leading governs how individual parts move from chord to chord in polyphonic texture.

Principles

Prefer small steps. Retain common tones between chords. Use contrary motion between outer voices (soprano down, bass up).

Classical rules

Parallel perfect fifths and octaves between independent voices weaken line independence. In trad, octave doubling of melody is normal; parallel fifths in two harmony instruments can hollow texture.

Session application

Bass moves by step or fifth when possible. Harmony on strong beats; passing motion on weak beats.

Quiz Questions

Q1. Smooth voice leading prefers:

  • A) Small steps and common tones
  • B) Large leaps always
  • C) Parallel octaves always
  • D) Random chord tones

Answer: A

Q2. Contrary motion: one voice rises, another falls.

Answer: True
