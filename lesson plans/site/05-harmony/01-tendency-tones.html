Tendency Tones and Resolution

Track: harmony | Difficulty: 4/10 | Time: ~10 min

Overview

Certain pitches in tonal music ‘want’ to move to others, creating the feeling of arrival or suspense.

Leading tone

7th scale degree in major rises to tonic by semitone. In G major, F♯ wants to resolve up to G.

Tritone resolution

The tritone in V7 (B and F in G7→C) resolves outward: B up to C, F down to E. This defines dominant function.

Melodic tendency

2̂ often steps to 1̂ or 3̂. 4̂ may fall to 3̂. Backing musicians who clash with unresolved melody tendency sound wrong even on ‘correct’ chords.

Quiz Questions

Q1. Leading tone resolves:

  • A) Up to tonic by semitone
  • B) Down to subdominant
  • C) Up by whole tone to mediant
  • D) No preferred direction

Answer: A

Q2. V7 tritone creates tension resolving at cadence.

Answer: True
