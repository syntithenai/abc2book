Transposing Chord Progressions

Track: transposition | Difficulty: 4/10 | Time: ~8 min

Overview

Roman numerals and Nashville numbers make chord transposition systematic.

By letter

G-D-Em-C up a whole step = A-E-F♯m-D. Move every symbol the same distance.

Nashville numbers

1-5-6m-4 in D = D-A-Bm-G; in G = G-D-Em-C. Session guitarists call changes by number when singers shift key.

Non-diatonic chords

Borrowed ♭VII and secondary dominants transpose with the same interval. Capo: shapes in C with capo 2 sounds in D.

Quiz Questions

Q1. I-V-vi-IV in C:

  • A) C-G-Am-F
  • B) G-C-D-Em
  • C) G-A-Bm-D
  • D) Em-C-G-D

Answer: A — and in G: G-D-Em-C.

Q2. Nashville numbers label by scale degree.

Answer: True
