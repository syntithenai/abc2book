Diction and Lyrics

Track: singing | Difficulty: 2/10 | Time: ~10 min

Overview

Clear diction communicates story; sloppy consonants lose words in noisy sessions and recordings.

Consonants

Articulate final consonants lightly — ‘t’ and ‘k’ without glottal stops unless stylistically intended. Irish and Scots Gaelic lyrics need language coaching for authentic pronunciation.

Vowels on long notes

Sustain on pure vowel; place consonant at end. ‘Love’ = ‘luh-uv’ not ‘luh-vuh’ spread across the note.

Microphone

Close-mic singing needs less consonant explosion than unamplified ballad in a hall.

Lyric sheets

Know meaning, not just sounds. Storytelling improves phrasing naturally.

Quiz Questions

Q1. Sustain long notes primarily on:

  • A) Pure vowel sound
  • B) Consonants
  • C) Silence
  • D) Breath noise

Answer: A

Q2. Gaelic song lyrics benefit from language-specific pronunciation guidance.

Answer: True
