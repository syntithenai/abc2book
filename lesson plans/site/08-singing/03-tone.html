Tone Production

Track: singing | Difficulty: 2/10 | Time: ~10 min

Overview

Tone colour — bright, dark, breathy, focused — comes from vocal tract shaping, breath pressure, and resonance balance.

Resonance

Chest resonance (lower notes), head resonance (higher). Mixed voice blends both. Forward placement (‘mask’ resonance) aids projection without pushing.

Vowel uniformity

Keep vowel shape consistent across pitch changes. ‘Ah’ widening at high notes spreads tone thin.

Folk context

Sean-nós embraces natural voice colour — not operatic uniformity. Match tone to song: lullaby vs drinking song vs lament.

Avoid

Pushing throat, excessive jaw tension, manufactured vibrato before basic pitch is stable.

Quiz Questions

Q1. Forward placement helps:

  • A) Project without throat pushing
  • B) Eliminate all resonance
  • C) Lower pitch by an octave
  • D) Replace breath support

Answer: A

Q2. Mixed voice blends chest and head resonance.

Answer: True
