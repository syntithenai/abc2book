# Cadences — Advanced and Historical

**Track:** theory | **Tier:** advanced | **Difficulty:** 7/10
**Prerequisites:** Cadences — Applied
**Tags:** cadences, baroque, jazz-turnaround

## Overview

Historical cadence types, **evaded** and **Phrygian** cadences, and jazz **turnarounds** as repeating cadential patterns.

## Phrygian half cadence

In minor, ♭II–V (bass step down) — Baroque and some art song. Distinct sound from diatonic half cadence.

## Jazz turnaround

I–vi–ii–V–I cycle extends cadential energy — representative of hundreds of standards, not every standard individually.

## Evaded cadence

Dominant resolves unexpectedly — analysis skill for Romantic repertoire.
