# Cadences — Applied

**Track:** theory | **Tier:** applied | **Difficulty:** 5/10
**Prerequisites:** Cadences — Introduction
**Tags:** cadences, phrase, deceptive

## Overview

Spot cadences in scores and tunes; connect them to **phrase structure** and bass lines.

## Phrase rhythm

Antecedent phrase often ends half cadence; consequent ends perfect. AABB tune forms map naturally to question–answer pairs.

## Inversions at cadence

I6–V or cadential 6/4 embellishes dominant before resolution — common in classical; simplified in folk.
