# Figured Bass and Continuo Practice

**Track:** theory-advanced | **Difficulty:** 5/10 | **Time:** ~12 min

## Overview

Figured bass (thoroughbass) is Baroque shorthand for harmony above a bass line — foundation of 17th–18th century accompaniment.

## Figures

Numbers below bass indicate intervals above bass note to play. 5/3 = root position triad. 6 = first inversion. 6/4 = second inversion. 7 = seventh chord. Accidentals alter figures.

## Realisation

Keyboardist improvises chord voicings from figures + bass. Harpsichord, organ, or lute with cello/bassoon on bass line.

## Modern relevance

Understanding figures clarifies Bach harmonic analysis. Jazz chord symbols are distant descendants. Roman numeral analysis partially replaces figures today.

## Quiz Questions

### Q1. Figured bass 6 below bass indicates:

- A) First inversion triad
- B) Root position
- C) Second inversion only
- D) Silence

**Answer:** A

### Q2. Continuo players realise harmonies from bass + figures.

**Answer:** True
