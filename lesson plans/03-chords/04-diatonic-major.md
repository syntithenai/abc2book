# Diatonic Triads in Major Keys

**Track:** chords | **Difficulty:** 3/10 | **Time:** ~10 min

## Overview

Diatonic triads use only notes from the major scale, one chord per scale degree.

## Pattern

I (major), ii (minor), iii (minor), IV (major), V (major), vi (minor), vii° (diminished). In G major: G, Am, Bm, C, D, Em, F♯dim.

## Primary harmonies

I, IV, and V are the workhorses of folk song. vi (relative minor) adds emotional contrast. ii often precedes V in sophisticated progressions.

## Melody mapping

When guessing chords in D major, start with D, G, A, Bm. Melody notes on F♯ and A over a D bass suggest V.

## Quiz Questions

### Q1. vi chord in G major:

- A) Em
- B) Am
- C) Bm
- D) C

**Answer:** A

### Q2. Major key diatonic pattern: I ii iii IV V vi vii°

**Answer:** True
