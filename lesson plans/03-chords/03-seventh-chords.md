# Seventh Chords and Their Qualities

**Track:** chords | **Difficulty:** 4/10 | **Time:** ~10 min

## Overview

Adding a seventh above the root creates four-note chords with richer colour and stronger harmonic function.

## Types

**Major 7th (maj7)**: major triad + major 7th (C-E-G-B). **Dominant 7th (7)**: major triad + minor 7th (C-E-G-B♭) — V7 pulls to I. **Minor 7th (m7)**: minor triad + minor 7th (D-F-A-C). **Half-diminished (m7♭5)**: diminished triad + minor 7th. **Fully diminished (dim7)**: diminished triad + diminished 7th.

## Guide tones

The 3rd and 7th of a seventh chord define its function even when root is omitted. In G7, B and F are the tritone that resolves to C major.

## In trad

Dominant sevenths colour céilí band voicings; maj7 appears more in contemporary arrangements than strict session backup.

## Quiz Questions

### Q1. G7 contains which seventh above G?

- A) F (minor 7th)
- B) F♯
- C) E
- D) A

**Answer:** A

### Q2. V7 strongly pulls to I in major keys.

**Answer:** True
