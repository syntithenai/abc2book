# Diatonic Harmony in Minor Keys

**Track:** chords | **Difficulty:** 4/10 | **Time:** ~10 min

## Overview

Minor-key harmony shifts with natural, harmonic, and melodic minor scales.

## Natural minor

i, ii°, III, iv, v, VI, VII. In Am: Am, Bdim, C, Dm, Em, F, G. v is minor — weaker cadence.

## Harmonic minor

Raised 7th makes V major (E major in Am) with strong pull to i. III and VI provide modal pivots.

## Trad practice

Listen for raised 7th at cadences. Irish minor tunes blend aeolian and dorian without announcing which textbook scale they follow.

## Quiz Questions

### Q1. V chord in A harmonic minor:

- A) E major
- B) E minor
- C) Em7
- D) E diminished

**Answer:** A

### Q2. ii is diminished in natural minor.

**Answer:** True
