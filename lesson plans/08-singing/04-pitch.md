# Pitch and Intonation

**Track:** singing | **Difficulty:** 2/10 | **Time:** ~10 min

## Overview

Accurate pitch is non-negotiable in ensemble singing — listeners forgive tone colour before they forgive wrong notes.

## Listening

Hear the target pitch internally before singing. Reference instruments (fiddle, guitar) or other singers. Unaccompanied singing demands stronger internal pitch sense.

## Intonation

Equal temperament vs just intonation — major thirds slightly wide in ET. Blend with ensemble by listening, not forcing.

## Tools

Drone practice (tanpura, sustained chord, tuning app). Record yourself. Slow practice on difficult intervals.

## Trad tunes

Ornamentation must land on correct pitch — grace notes approach from below/above but resolve accurately.

## Quiz Questions

### Q1. Internal audiation means:

- A) Hearing the pitch before singing it
- B) Ignoring other musicians
- C) Singing random notes
- D) Only reading lyrics

**Answer:** A

### Q2. Unaccompanied singing requires strong internal pitch reference.

**Answer:** True
