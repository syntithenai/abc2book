# Expression and Storytelling

**Track:** singing | **Difficulty:** 3/10 | **Time:** ~10 min

## Overview

Expression transforms correct notes into communication — the difference between reciting and telling.

## Dynamics and colour

Soft verses, louder chorus — or inverse for surprise. Vowel colour shifts mood: bright 'ee' vs dark 'oh'.

## Rubato in folk

Melody breathes within steady pulse — especially ballads and airs. Rhythm section holds tempo while singer flexes.

## Narrative arc

Know who speaks, what happened, why it matters. First verse sets scene; final verse lands emotional point.

## Variation

Second time through: subtle dynamic or tempo shift, not random ornament. Serve the story.

## Quiz Questions

### Q1. Rubato in folk ballads:

- A) Flexible melody timing within steady pulse
- B) Ignoring rhythm entirely
- C) Speeding up every bar
- D) Eliminating all dynamics

**Answer:** A

### Q2. Understanding lyric meaning improves natural phrasing.

**Answer:** True
