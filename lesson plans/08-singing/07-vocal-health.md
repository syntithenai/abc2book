# Vocal Health

**Track:** singing | **Difficulty:** 1/10 | **Time:** ~10 min

## Overview

Your voice is irreplaceable equipment — protect it like a fiddler protects their hands.

## Hydration

Water before and during gigs. Alcohol and caffeine dehydrate vocal folds. Steam inhalation helps before singing.

## Rest

Avoid shouting over noisy pubs before sets. Schedule vocal rest days after demanding gigs.

## Warm-up and cool-down

Gentle hum descending after performance. Don't go from full belt to silence.

## Warning signs

Hoarseness >2 weeks, pain, loss of range — see ENT specialist. Nodules from chronic misuse are preventable.

## Environment

Dry air, smoke, and allergens irritate folds. Humidifier in hotel rooms on tour.

## Quiz Questions

### Q1. Persistent hoarseness over two weeks warrants:

- A) Medical evaluation
- B) Singing louder
- C) Ignoring it
- D) Only whispering forever

**Answer:** A

### Q2. Hydration supports healthy vocal fold function.

**Answer:** True

### Q3. Vocal cool-down after gig:

- A) Gentle descending hums
- B) Immediate maximum-volume singing
- C) Complete dehydration
- D) Shouting celebrations

**Answer:** A
