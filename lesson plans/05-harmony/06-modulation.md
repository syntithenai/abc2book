# Modulation: Changing Key Mid-Tune

**Track:** harmony | **Difficulty:** 8/10 | **Time:** ~10 min

## Overview

Modulation establishes a new tonic, not merely touching foreign chords.

## Common moves

Up a whole step (G→A) for energy. To relative minor (G→Em). Pivot chord shared by two keys (C major = IV in G, I in C).

## Detection

Sustained new accidentals + cadence confirming new tonic = modulation. Single A7 in D = tonicisation; four bars in A with A cadence = modulation.

## Trad context

Less common in core repertoire than céilí arrangements and song verses.

## Quiz Questions

### Q1. Pivot-chord modulation uses:

- A) Chord belonging to both old and new keys
- B) Silence only
- C) Tempo change only
- D) Random accidentals

**Answer:** A

### Q2. Modulation establishes new tonic; tonicisation is brief.

**Answer:** True
