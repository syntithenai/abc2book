# Counterpoint: Independent Melodic Lines

**Track:** harmony | **Difficulty:** 9/10 | **Time:** ~10 min

## Overview

Counterpoint combines melodies so each line remains intelligible while harmonising together.

## Species counterpoint

Gradus ad Parnassum tradition: stepwise motion, consonance on strong beats, careful dissonance treatment. Foundation for Bach fugues.

## Motion types

**Contrary**: voices move opposite directions. **Parallel**: same direction. **Oblique**: one holds, other moves. Contrary and oblique preserve independence.

## In trad

Pipe regulators, harp bass, twin-fiddle harmony. Lines need not share rhythm. Sing each line alone — if it sounds random, simplify.

## Quiz Questions

### Q1. Counterpoint's goal:

- A) Independent coherent melodic lines combined
- B) Unison always
- C) No dissonance ever
- D) Percussion only

**Answer:** A

### Q2. Contrary motion: one ascends, other descends.

**Answer:** True
