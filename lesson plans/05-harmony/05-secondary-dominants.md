# Secondary Dominants and Tonicisation

**Track:** harmony | **Difficulty:** 7/10 | **Time:** ~10 min

## Overview

Secondary dominants temporarily treat a non-tonic chord as a local key centre.

## Labelling

A7 in C major = V7/vi (resolves to Dm). D7 = V7/V (resolves to G). Accidentals foreign to home key signature signal their presence.

## In repertoire

O'Carolan arrangements, ragtime hornpipes, contemporary folk songwriting. V7/vi→vi brightens briefly before returning to I.

## Detection

Sudden sharpened note outside key for one bar? Suspect secondary dominant or modal mixture.

## Quiz Questions

### Q1. V7/vi in C major:

- A) A7
- B) E7
- C) G7
- D) F7

**Answer:** A

### Q2. Secondary dominants introduce foreign accidentals.

**Answer:** True
