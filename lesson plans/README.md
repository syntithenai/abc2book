# Music Theory Lesson Plans

A sequenced curriculum covering notation, harmony, history, instruments, styles, and vocal technique. Each document is self-contained markdown suitable for classroom presentation, self-study, or AI-assisted quiz and article generation.

## How to use this curriculum

Work through tracks in order within each section. Prerequisites are listed at the top of each lesson. Difficulty runs from 0 (absolute beginner) to 10 (advanced conservatory level). Estimated reading time does not include practice time.

## Source material

Lessons draw on standard music theory pedagogy, the app's existing Knowledge Feed curriculum, and reference material including Wikipedia articles on instruments, composers, and music history. Facts are condensed for teaching; verify performance-practice details against specialist sources when preparing advanced coursework.

## Quiz format

Each lesson ends with multiple-choice and true/false questions. Answers and brief explanations follow each question block.
