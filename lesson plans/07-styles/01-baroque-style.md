# Baroque Style: Ornament, Continuo, and Dance

**Track:** styles | **Difficulty:** 4/10 | **Time:** ~10 min

## Overview

Baroque music (c. 1600–1750) features continuous motion, basso continuo, expected ornamentation, and dance-suite organisation.

## Continuo

Keyboard and lute realise harmonies from a bass line with figured-bass numbers. Melody instruments play above this foundation.

## Ornamentation

Trills, mordents, appoggiaturas expected — especially on cadences and repeats. Baroque treatises (Quantz, C.P.E. Bach) document practice.

## Dance suites

Allemande, courante, sarabande, gigue — court dances structuring instrumental music. O'Carolan and baroque flute arrangements inhabit this world.
