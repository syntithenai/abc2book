# Jazz Basics: Swing, Standards, and Changes

**Track:** styles | **Difficulty:** 5/10 | **Time:** ~10 min

## Overview

Jazz centres on swing feel, improvisation, and chord-progression literacy — grown from blues, ragtime, and brass band traditions.

## Lead sheets

Melody + chord symbols; arrangement and soloing improvised. Standards (*Autumn Leaves*, *All the Things You Are*) shared like session tune titles.

## Harmony

ii–V–I, tritone substitutions, turnarounds everywhere. Rhythm section: walking bass, offbeat comping, ride cymbal swing.

## Folk crossover

Jazz voicings on song accompaniment; improvised choruses between tune repeats. Outline roots on strong beats first.
