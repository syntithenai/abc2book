# Romantic Style: Expression and Expanded Harmony

**Track:** styles | **Difficulty:** 5/10 | **Time:** ~10 min

## Overview

19th-century Romanticism stretches forms for emotional narrative — bigger orchestras, chromatic harmony, virtuoso solo writing.

## Harmony

Borrowed chords, augmented sixths, extended dominants colour mood. Nationalist composers (Dvořák, Grieg, Sibelius) embed folk dances in symphonic works.

## In trad

Lush piano intros to airs, dramatic key changes in céilí sets, vibrato-heavy string treatment. Use Romantic tools where story needs shadow — not every reel wants a Rachmaninoff ending.

## Quiz Questions

### Q1. Romantic music characterised by:

- A) Expanded expression and richer harmony
- B) No dynamics
- C) Plainchant only
- D) No solos

**Answer:** A

### Q2. Nationalist Romantics incorporated folk material.

**Answer:** True
