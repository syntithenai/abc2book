# Cape Breton Fiddle and Dance

**Track:** Celtic Music | **Unit:** Diaspora & fringe | **Tier:** 3

## Overview

Cape Breton fiddle style synthesises Scottish and Irish roots with step-dance rhythm and square/set dance culture in Nova Scotia.

## Compared with Ireland

Irish diaspora scenes (New York, Chicago, London) exported session culture and tune repertoire worldwide. This lesson traces how **cape breton fiddle and dance** relates to that network while developing its own accent.

## Key points

- Diaspora traditions remix Irish and Scottish roots with local dance floors and radio.
- Comparative listening prevents mistaking one region's accent for the whole "Celtic" label.
- Festivals and archives connect fringe regions to the pan-Celtic circuit.
