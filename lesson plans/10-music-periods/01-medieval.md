# Medieval Music (500–1400)

**Track:** music-periods | **Difficulty:** 3/10 | **Time:** ~12 min

## Overview

Medieval music spans from fall of Rome through the Black Death to early Renaissance — monophonic chant to early polyphony.

## Gregorian chant

Monophonic, unmeasured sacred Latin song — foundation of Western notation. Neumes evolved into staff notation by Guido d'Arezzo (11th century).

## Early polyphony

Organum: parallel voices added to chant. Notre Dame school (Léonin, Pérotin) — 12th-13th century measured rhythm. Motets combined sacred and secular texts.

## Instruments

Lute, harp, vielle (fiddle predecessor), recorder, bagpipe. Minstrels and troubadours performed secular song in courts.
