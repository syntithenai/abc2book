# Renaissance Music (1400–1600)

**Track:** music-periods | **Difficulty:** 3/10 | **Time:** ~12 min

## Overview

Renaissance brought humanism, printing, and golden-age polyphony — Palestrina, Josquin, Byrd.

## Polyphony

Independent vocal lines woven in imitative counterpoint. Mass settings and madrigals. Consonance rules refined; dissonance on weak beats.

## Instruments

Lute flourished; viol consort; early keyboard. Dance music published in collections (Attaignant, Praetorius).

## Reformation impact

Lutheran chorales and Anglican hymnody shaped congregational singing — ancestors of folk hymn tradition.
