# Classical Era Overview (1750–1820)

**Track:** music-periods | **Difficulty:** 3/10 | **Time:** ~12 min

## Overview

Classical era: clarity, balance, sonata form. Haydn, Mozart, early Beethoven.

## Ensemble

String quartet crystallised as conversation between equals. Symphony orchestra standardised sections.

## Public concerts

Music moved from court patronage toward public ticket sales — amateur music-making in homes.

## Piano replaces harpsichord

Dynamic nuance of fortepiano suited Classical expressiveness.
