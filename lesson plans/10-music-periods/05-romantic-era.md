# Romantic Era Overview (1810–1900)

**Track:** music-periods | **Difficulty:** 3/10 | **Time:** ~12 min

## Overview

Romanticism: individual expression, nationalism, expanded orchestra, programme music.

## Composers

Chopin, Schumann, Liszt, Brahms, Wagner, Tchaikovsky, Mahler. Symphonies grow to 80+ minutes.

## Nationalism

Folk melodies in concert works legitimised oral tradition for middle-class audiences.

## Virtuosity

Paganini, Liszt — performer-composers as celebrities. Virtuoso tradition continues in trad festival competition.

## Quiz Questions

### Q1. Romantic era emphasised:

- A) Personal expression and nationalism
- B) Strict church modes only
- C) No orchestras
- D) Unmeasured chant

**Answer:** A
