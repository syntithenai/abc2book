# Learn hero image attribution

## Themed heroes (generated)

Paths: `public/learn/{vanilla|celtic|hindu|sunrise}/{slug}.jpg`.

All themes are **photorealistic** Comfy Flux.2 Klein photography with theme atmosphere:
- **vanilla:** neutral studio/home light
- **celtic:** cool moss/oak outdoor or stone/linen light (not folk ornament)
- **hindu:** warm marigold–indigo South Asian practice setting (not Madhubani borders)
- **sunrise:** fiery coral-gold / molten gold against deep teal–indigo (high saturation, not pastel wash)
- **sunrise2:** photoreal golden-hour photography — coral-gold light, deep teal shadows, heron/horizon motifs as real scenes

Manifest: `scripts/drafts/learn_image_manifest.json`. Generator: `scripts/generate_editorial_images.py`.
